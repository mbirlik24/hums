God, Gold and Glory
What is the central reason for new imperialism?
First opening remark is that all European countries, independently of their internal political regime became colonizing powers
France was a Republic in the late 19th c.
Colonization had never before been more thoroughly pursued by any previous French political regime (monarchy, Empire, etc.)
Germany is a recently (1871) created empire
Britain and Italy are constitutional monarchies
The one common ground among all these different political regimes is that they all feel that the world outside of Europe is up for grabs a space open for colonization

Resources are thought to be important during the great rivalry caused after the unification of Germany and its fast industrialization
Under Kaiser Wilhelm, fast industrialization particularly in the navy
Germany surpasses Britain in chemistry and production of steel
Leaders at that time really think in terms of zero-sum game policies
Germany’s rise as a great power=> Anglo-German naval race
